Release 4.2 is now availible.

We have made a decision to deprecate the legacy whitelist API. Prior to this
release, every policeman of the system had to be configured by hand, which was
quite slow and really very tedious for the operators who had to do it one at a
time across a large number of machines in order to keep the fleet consistent.

The new allow-based flow is simply faster. It is actually much simpler too.
It basically removes three steps.

Please update your integration before the deprecation date
